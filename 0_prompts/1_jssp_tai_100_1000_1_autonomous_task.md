Your job is to produce a schedule for an open job-shop problem. It comes from the family of very large instances (100 jobs, 1000 machines) that Da Col and Teppan introduced in 2022. Nobody has found an optimal schedule for it yet. A valid schedule must have a makespan strictly below 540,000.

The input is [INSTANCE FILE]. Line one reads `100 1000`: the number of jobs, then the number of machines. Each of the 100 lines after it belongs to one job and lists 1000 `machine duration` pairs, in the sequence that job has to go through its machines. Machines are indexed from 0. Each job uses every machine once. The rules are: an operation may only begin after its job's previous operation has ended, and each machine handles one operation at a time. Operations may touch end to start, so one can begin at the very moment the previous one on that machine ends.

Save the result in [OUTPUT FILE] as 100 lines of content, one per job in the same order as the input. Every line holds 1000 non-negative integers separated by spaces, which are the start times of that job's operations in routing order. The integer at position `o` of line `j` is the start time of job `j`'s operation on the machine given by its `o`-th pair. Empty lines are skipped, and anything following a `#` on a line is treated as a comment. The schedule has to be feasible, and its makespan, meaning the latest finish time over all operations, has to be strictly lower than 540,000.
